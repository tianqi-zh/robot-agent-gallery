# Evaluation methodology

The full benchmark protocols and gallery export documentation now live with the independent [Hugging Face gallery](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Alan0928/robot-agent-gallery).

- [Benchmark protocols and historical import evidence](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Alan0928/robot-agent-gallery/blob/main/METHODOLOGY.md)
- [Training demonstration sources](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Alan0928/robot-agent-gallery/blob/main/TRAINING_DEMOS.md)
- [Complete evaluation archive and CSV](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Alan0928/robot-agent-gallery/tree/main/data)
- [Essay accounting, reviewed labels, and limitations](LIBERO_ALIGNMENT.md)

The current gallery presents 1,286 scored episodes from 497 tasks. The archive also retains the 50 older Robotwin episodes. Training references and diagnostic reruns are separate from evaluation totals. Each benchmark reports its native outcome; different task distributions, controls, and budgets prevent pooling success rates.
