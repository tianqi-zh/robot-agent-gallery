HOW TO READ THIS GALLERY

The task is visual.
The score is native.

Both evaluations use GPT-6-Astra with high reasoning. Each episode starts with a fresh context and receives its own instruction, RGB camera views, robot state and calibration.

Read the evaluation protocol ↗
All scored episodes are included

400 LIBERO videos and 50 RoboTwin videos, including failures and one RoboTwin timeout. Final attempts follow infrastructure-only retries; policy failures were not retried to improve the score.

Success comes from the simulator

Results use each benchmark’s native success condition, not the agent’s written assessment. The policy receives no object ground-truth poses, reward or success labels, demonstrations, or cross-episode memory.

Playback and control time are different

LIBERO includes initial and warmup frames, then every control step at 20 FPS. RoboTwin shows the initial frame and each native action’s final view at 10 FPS; these actions have variable physical duration. Thinking time is omitted. Web copies retain all recorded frames and camera views.

A first pass, not a universal score

LIBERO uses ten initial states per task. RoboTwin currently has one valid episode per task. Different tasks and protocols prevent direct comparison or pooling across benchmarks.
